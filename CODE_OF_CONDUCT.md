# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Whatstir to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity, experience or ability. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented) agrees to help keep it that way.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Respect differing opinions, and accept the maintainers' decisions on scope.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code. They may also ban a contributor, temporarily or permanently, for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/shivamshinde123). Every report will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
