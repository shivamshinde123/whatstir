Field notes

How an answer is grown.

  1. Load the directory

    Company data from the open-source yc-oss mirror goes into Postgres, with pgvector embeddings and AI-written problem groups per sector.

  2. Choose a scope

    A sector, a batch, a problem group, or companies you picked. The scope travels with every question in the session.

  3. Answer from descriptions only

    Retrieval finds the relevant companies and the model answers from their own words. A schema check keeps every citation pointing at a real company.

  4. Reach past the data

    When a question is about news or funding, a second call searches the web through OpenRouter and returns a labelled section with links.
